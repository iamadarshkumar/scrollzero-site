Social media without the doomscroll.

Keep the parts you need. Lose the parts that keep you hooked.

What it does

  • Endless feeds, switched off. Use Instagram without Reels and the Explore grid. Search, profiles and the people you follow still work.
  • Messages stay open. DMs, posting and stories work as usual. A reel a friend sends plays on its own, with no swipe to the next one.
  • A daily CatchUp instead. A short brief of what everyone is talking about. Read it, and you're done.
  • Private by design. No ScrollZero account, no ads, no tracking. You sign in to platforms directly, and your browsing stays on your phone.
