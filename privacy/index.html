Privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

In short

  • ScrollZero has no account, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.
  • We don't collect, store, sell or share your personal data. We don't run a server that receives it.
  • Your settings and your logins to platforms like Instagram stay on your device.
  • The app downloads a few public files (app updates, a settings file and the daily brief). Those requests don't identify you.

Who we are

ScrollZero is made by ScrollZero Studios (“we”, “us”), an independent developer. The app wraps social platforms such as Instagram in a focused browser and adds a short daily brief called CatchUp. Contact: support contact coming soon.

What stays on your device

ScrollZero saves a small amount of information on your device so the app works the way you set it up:

  • your shield choices (for example, whether the Explore grid is hidden);
  • whether you've seen the intro;
  • the last daily brief and the last app settings file it downloaded, so they work offline.

None of this is sent to us. It's deleted when you uninstall the app.

Platforms you use inside ScrollZero

When you open Instagram in ScrollZero, you connect directly to Instagram, just as you would in a web browser. Your login, cookies and browsing data for Instagram are stored on your device by your phone's built-in web view. ScrollZero never sees your password and doesn't send your messages, posts, contacts or activity anywhere.

To hide feeds, Reels, the Explore grid and sponsored posts, ScrollZero runs small scripts inside the Instagram page on your device. They only change what you see; they don't collect or send anything.

What Instagram collects while you use it is governed by Instagram's own privacy policy. You can sign out of Instagram inside the app at any time.

Network requests ScrollZero makes itself

Besides the platforms you choose to open, the app makes only these requests. None of them include your name, account, contacts or anything you do in the app.

Like any website, these providers may log technical details of a request, such as your IP address and the time, under their own privacy policies (Expo, GitHub). We don't receive or use those logs to identify you.

Links that open elsewhere

A story in the daily brief can open a related Instagram reel inside the app, and some links open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

ScrollZero is meant for people aged 18 and over and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Change or switch off shields any time on the Shield screen.
  • Sign out of Instagram inside the app, or clear its data by uninstalling ScrollZero.
  • Since we don't hold personal data about you, there's nothing for us to export or delete. If you have a question or a request, write to us.

Changes to this policy

If ScrollZero starts handling data differently (for example, if we add optional crash reporting), we'll update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
